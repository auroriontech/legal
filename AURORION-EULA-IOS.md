## 3. Data Collection and Use

### 3.1 Voice Data and Transcription
The App uses on-device speech recognition to transcribe your voice into text. Voice audio is processed locally on your device and is not recorded, stored, or transmitted.

The transcribed text is used for:
- AI-powered conversation and tour guidance
- Providing contextual responses to your queries
- Improving user experience

Only the text transcription (not voice recordings) may be transmitted to third-party AI service providers (including Azure OpenAI) for processing conversational responses.

### 3.2 Location Data
The App collects precise location data including GPS coordinates and address information (such as street, city, region, and country) to:
- Provide location-specific tour information and guidance
- Identify nearby points of interest
- Deliver contextual responses based on your current location

Location data is transmitted to our servers and AI service providers to generate personalized tour experiences. Location data is used in real-time and may be stored as part of your tour history.

### 3.3 User Account Data
When you create an account, we collect and store:
- Email address and authentication credentials
- Profile information
- Tour history and conversation messages

Account data is synchronized with our cloud backend service (Cloudflare D1 and R2) for cross-device access and data persistence.

### 3.4 Usage and Diagnostic Data
The App may collect:
- Error logs and crash reports
- App usage statistics (such as features used, session duration)
- Device information (including device model, operating system version, app version)
- Performance metrics

This data is used solely to improve app functionality, identify and fix technical issues, and enhance user experience.

### 3.5 How We Use Your Tour Data
We review tour history and conversation data for the following purposes:
- **Compliance**: Ensuring content adheres to our community guidelines and legal requirements
- **Safety**: Identifying and addressing potentially harmful or inappropriate content
- **Service Improvement**: Analyzing usage patterns to enhance tour quality and app features

This internal review is conducted by Aurorion staff and automated systems. Your personal information is handled in accordance with UK GDPR data protection standards.

### 3.6 Data Sharing with Partner Institutions
If you use tours featuring guides or characters provided by partner institutions (such as museums, universities, or cultural organizations), we may share your tour history and conversation data with that specific partner institution, but **only for tours involving their guides or characters**.

For example:
- If you take a tour with a museum's guide, that museum may receive data about your tour experience with their guide
- Tours taken with other guides or characters are **not** shared with that institution
- General tours without institutional partnerships are **not** shared with any third party

Partner institutions use this data to improve their content and understand visitor engagement. Data sharing with partners is clearly disclosed when you select their guides or characters.

### 3.7 Legal Basis for Processing
Processing of your data is based on your consent or, where applicable, our legitimate interest in providing and improving the service. Under UK GDPR, we process your data to:
- Fulfill our contract with you (delivering tour services)
- Comply with legal obligations
- Pursue legitimate interests (service improvement, fraud prevention, content safety, and enabling partner institutions to improve their content)
