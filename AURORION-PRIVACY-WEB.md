We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency in our data practices. This policy complies with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and UK data protection laws.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1.1 Account Information
When you create an account or sign in, we collect:
• Email address
• Full name
• Password (encrypted and hashed)
• Profile picture (optional)
• OAuth account connections (Google, Apple)
• Passkey credentials (WebAuthn standard)
• Account creation timestamp
• Last login timestamp

1.2 Location Data
We collect location information to provide location-based services:
• Precise GPS coordinates (when you grant location permission)
• IP address (for approximate geolocation - country/region level)
• Address information (street, city, region, country)
• Location history (when you use location-based features)
• Nearby points of interest

Location data is used to:
• Provide region-appropriate content and services
• Route you to the nearest server infrastructure for optimal performance
• Generate personalized location-based recommendations
• Enable real-time guided exploration features

IP addresses are processed in real-time and not stored long-term. Precise location data is stored only when you actively use location-based features.

1.3 Voice Data
When you use voice features, we collect:
• Voice transcriptions (text converted from speech)
• TTS audio files (AI-generated speech for playback of guide/character voices)
• Voice interaction analytics (usage patterns, frequency)

User voice audio is processed in real-time and NOT recorded or stored.

Voice data is used to:
• Provide voice-guided exploration experiences
• Generate AI-powered conversational responses
• Enable playback of guide/character voices (TTS)
• Improve transcription accuracy

You can manage voice consent through Service settings:
• Voice analytics (track voice interactions)
• Voice transcription (convert speech to text)
• TTS storage (store AI-generated audio for playback)

1.4 Conversation and Message Data
We store conversation data to provide and improve the Service:
• Conversation messages (text content, role, timestamp)
• Conversation metadata (title, type, participant information)
• Message attachments (files uploaded to conversations)
• Read receipts and typing indicators
• Conversation history and archives
• AI-generated responses and recommendations

Conversations may include:
• Interactions with AI guides and characters
• User-to-user messages (if applicable)
• System notifications and updates

1.5 User Preferences and Settings
We store your preferences to personalize your experience:
• Default guide or character selection
• Theme preferences (light, dark, system)
• Language settings
• Preferred interface mode (voice vs text)
• Notification preferences (email, push, in-app)
• Privacy settings (analytics, location sharing)
• User interests (history, science, art, technology)
• Exploration style (adventurous, methodical, social, independent)
• Onboarding completion status

1.6 Analytics and Usage Data
We collect anonymous usage analytics to improve the Service:

PostHog Analytics (Anonymous):
• Page views and screen views
• Button clicks and user interactions
• Feature usage patterns
• Session duration and frequency
• User flow and navigation paths
• Performance metrics

PostHog does NOT collect:
• Personally identifiable information (PII)
• IP addresses (masked automatically)
• Precise user identity

Microsoft Clarity (Anonymous):
• Session recordings (heatmaps, click maps, scroll maps)
• User behavior patterns
• Website performance metrics
• Error tracking

Microsoft Clarity does NOT collect:
• Personally identifiable information (automatically masked)
• IP addresses (hashed for privacy)
• Sensitive form inputs (automatically redacted)

You can opt out of analytics tracking at any time through our privacy settings.

1.7 Technical and Device Information
We automatically collect technical data when you use the Service:
• Browser type and version (e.g., Chrome 120, Safari 17)
• Browser capabilities (supported web features, WebGL, etc.)
• Operating system and version
• Device type (desktop, mobile, tablet)
• Screen resolution and viewport size
• Referrer URL (where you came from)
• User agent string
• IP address (for geolocation and security)
• Timestamp of access

This data is used to:
• Ensure browser compatibility
• Optimize content delivery for your device
• Identify and fix browser-specific issues
• Prioritize feature development
• Detect and prevent fraud

1.8 Cookies and Similar Technologies
We use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience:

Essential Cookies (Always Required):
• Session management (aurorion-session, aurorion-dev-session)
• Authentication (aurorion-auth-token)
• Security and CSRF protection
• Load balancing and performance

Analytics Cookies (Opt-In):
• PostHog cookies (posthog-*) for usage analytics
• Microsoft Clarity cookies (clarity-*) for session recordings

Preference Cookies:
• Theme preferences (light/dark mode)
• Language and region settings
• Cookie consent preferences
• Analytics opt-out flags

You can manage cookie preferences through:
• Our cookie consent banner (first visit)
• Privacy settings in your account
• Browser settings (may affect functionality)

1.9 Email Communications and Surveys
When you subscribe to our Pioneer Circle or newsletter, we collect:
• Email address
• Subscription timestamp
• Subscription source (landing page, referral, etc.)
• Email open and click rates (to improve communication)

We may occasionally send surveys and forms to gather feedback:
• Survey responses (optional participation)
• Feedback about product features
• Travel preferences and interests
• Business model research questions

All surveys are:
• Completely optional (no penalty for not participating)
• Clearly marked (anonymous vs identifiable)
• Not shared with third parties for marketing purposes
• Used solely for business development and service improvement

1.10 Consent Records
We maintain detailed consent records for GDPR compliance:
• Consent domain (user features, marketing, legal)
• Consent feature (voice, messages, location, profile)
• Consent capability (analytics, storage, sharing)
• Consent timestamp and method (checkbox, banner, settings)
• IP address and user agent at time of consent
• Consent version and updates
• Withdrawal timestamp (if applicable)
• Full consent history (audit trail)

You have the right to view, update, or withdraw any consent at any time.

3. LEGAL BASIS FOR PROCESSING (GDPR)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Under GDPR Article 6, we process your personal data based on the following legal grounds:

3.1 Consent (Article 6(1)(a))
• Email marketing communications (opt-in)
• Analytics cookies (opt-in via cookie banner)
• Voice transcription and TTS storage (explicit consent)
• Location tracking (explicit permission)
• Survey participation (voluntary consent)

You can withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing based on consent before withdrawal.

3.2 Contract Performance (Article 6(1)(b))
• Creating and managing your account
• Providing the Service you requested
• Processing transactions and subscriptions
• Enabling voice-guided exploration features
• Synchronizing your data across devices

3.3 Legitimate Interest (Article 6(1)(f))
• IP-based location optimization (improve service delivery)
• Browser compatibility detection (ensure optimal experience)
• Fraud prevention and security monitoring
• Service improvement and analytics (aggregated data)
• Customer support and inquiries

We balance our legitimate interests against your rights and freedoms. You have the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests.

3.4 Legal Obligation (Article 6(1)(c))
• Complying with tax and accounting requirements
• Responding to lawful requests from authorities
• Maintaining records for legal compliance
• Data protection and security obligations

• Backups retained for up to 90 days for disaster recovery
• Deleted data may persist in backups until backup rotation completes

6.2 Legal Retention
Despite deletion requests, we may retain certain data when:
• Required by law (tax, accounting, legal hold)
• Necessary to resolve disputes or enforce agreements

7. YOUR RIGHTS AND CONTROLS

7.1 GDPR Rights
Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the following rights:

Right of Access (Article 15):
• Request a copy of your personal data
• Receive information about how we process your data
• Learn which third parties have received your data

Right to Rectification (Article 16):
• Request correction of inaccurate data
• Update incomplete information

Right to Erasure (Article 17) - "Right to be Forgotten":
• Request complete deletion of your personal data
• We will process deletion requests within 30 days
• Some data may be retained for legal compliance

Right to Restriction of Processing (Article 18):
• Request limitation of how we process your data
• Temporarily suspend processing while disputing accuracy

Right to Data Portability (Article 20):
• Receive your data in structured, machine-readable format (JSON)
• Transfer your data to another service provider

Right to Object (Article 21):
• Object to processing based on legitimate interests
• Opt out of direct marketing at any time
• Object to profiling and automated decision-making

Right to Withdraw Consent (Article 7):
• Withdraw consent at any time for consent-based processing
• Does not affect lawfulness of processing before withdrawal

Right to Lodge a Complaint:
• File a complaint with your local Data Protection Authority
• UK: Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) - https://ico.org.uk

7.2 Exercising Your Rights
To exercise any of these rights, contact us at:
privacy@aurorion.app

7.6 Opt Out of Analytics

8. DATA PROTECTION AND SECURITY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

8.1 Security Measures
We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

Technical Safeguards:
• Encryption in transit (TLS 1.3 for all connections)
• Encryption at rest (Cloudflare D1, R2, KV encryption)
• Password hashing (bcrypt with salt)
• Secure session management (httpOnly, secure, sameSite cookies)
• Two-factor authentication support (Passkeys, OAuth)

Organizational Safeguards:
• Access controls (role-based access, least privilege principle)
• Regular security audits and penetration testing
• Employee training on data protection
• Incident response procedures
• Data processing agreements with all vendors

Application Security:
• CSRF protection on all state-changing requests
• XSS prevention (input sanitization, output encoding)
• SQL injection prevention (parameterized queries via Drizzle ORM)
• Rate limiting to prevent abuse
• Content Security Policy (CSP) headers

8.2 Data Breach Notification
In the event of a data breach affecting your personal data:
• We will notify you within 72 hours (GDPR requirement)
• Notification will include nature of breach and affected data
• We will provide guidance on protective measures
• We will report to relevant Data Protection Authorities

8.3 Limitations
Despite our security measures:
• No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure
• We cannot guarantee absolute security
• You are responsible for maintaining account security
• Use strong passwords and enable two-factor authentication

If you suspect unauthorized access to your account, contact us immediately at security@aurorion.app.

9. COOKIES AND SIMILAR TECHNOLOGIES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

9.1 What Are Cookies?
Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit our website. They help us remember your preferences, analyze usage, and provide personalized experiences.

9.2 Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies (Always Active):
These cookies are necessary for the Service to function:
• Session management (aurorion-session, aurorion-dev-session)
• Authentication (aurorion-auth-token)
• Security tokens (CSRF protection)
• Load balancing
• Cookie consent preferences

Analytics Cookies (Opt-In):
These cookies help us understand how you use the Service:
• PostHog cookies (posthog-*) - Usage analytics, feature tracking
• Microsoft Clarity cookies (clarity-*) - Session recordings, heatmaps

You can opt out via cookie banner or privacy settings.

Preference Cookies:
These cookies remember your choices:
• Theme preferences (light/dark mode)
• Language and region settings
• Analytics opt-out status

9.3 Cookie Lifespan
• Session cookies: Deleted when you close your browser
• Persistent cookies: 1-2 years (or until you delete them)
• Authentication cookies: 30 days (or until logout)

9.4 Managing Cookies
You can control cookies through:
• Our cookie consent banner (first visit)
• Privacy settings in your account
• Browser settings (varies by browser):
  - Chrome: Settings > Privacy > Cookies
  - Firefox: Settings > Privacy > Cookies
  - Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Cookies
  - Edge: Settings > Privacy > Cookies

Note: Blocking essential cookies will prevent you from using the Service.

9.5 Third-Party Cookies
We use third-party cookies from:
• PostHog (analytics) - posthog.com
• Microsoft Clarity (analytics) - clarity.ms
• Google OAuth (authentication) - google.com
• Apple OAuth (authentication) - apple.com

These cookies are subject to the third parties' privacy policies.
